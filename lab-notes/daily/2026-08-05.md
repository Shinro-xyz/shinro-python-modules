### 2026-08-05 15:10 UTC — integration test environment brought to passing + CI independence

**What changed**

Completed and verified the full-loop integration test environment under `tests/integration/` (scaffold first landed in `ba2f495` on this branch; this session fixed the blockers and finished the wiring).

- **`tests/integration/`** — 5 test modules + `conftest.py` + `helpers/` + 4 scenario TOMLs, all gated behind `pytest.mark.integration`:
  - `test_base_tracking.py` — full closed loop Trajectory→Controller→Estimator→HolonomicMobileRobot→MuJoCo; parametrized over LQR/LQR+Luenberger/MPC_LTI/MPC_DeltaU.
  - `test_arm_cartesian.py` — PID+Kalman tracking a 6D end-effector lift schedule on the SO-ARM100.
  - `test_pick_and_place.py` — `phase_list` multi-plant feedforward (base+arm+jaw) through `RobotSim`.
  - `test_adversarial_integration.py` — NaN measurement injected at step 50 must raise, not corrupt control.
  - `test_scenario_factory.py` — construction validation + dimension-mismatch errors.
- **`helpers/scenario_runner.py`** — `run_scenario` (closed loop, single plant) and `run_phase_schedule` (feedforward multi-signal). Raises `ValueError` when the estimator propagates NaN/Inf — this is the agreed adversarial contract.
- **`factories/scenario_factory.py`** — clear `KeyError` for unknown plant names; optional controller/estimator sections (feedforward pick-and-place scenarios).
- **`configs/controllers/mpc_lti_base.toml`** — fixed a latent constraint-dimension bug: F = [I; −I] is 6 rows, so upper/lower bounds must be length 6 (was 3 → OSQP `Incorrect dimension of l`).
- **`configs/estimators/kalman_arm.toml`, `configs/plants/holonomic_base.toml`, `configs/trajectories/arm_lift.toml`** — arm estimator, base plant, and 6D arm lift configs.
- **CI independence**: `pyproject.toml` has `addopts = "-m 'not integration'"` so any `pytest` invocation skips the slow MuJoCo suite by default; `make test-integration` (or `run_tests.py --int`) opts in with `--override-ini="addopts="`. `tests/integration/conftest.py` adds a second safety net — `pytest.importorskip("mujoco")` + asset-existence check.
- **Removed** root `test_pick_and_place.py` (legacy, non-pytest, bypassed controllers/estimators) and the `test-pick-and-place` Makefile target. Old behavior is subsumed by `tests/integration/test_pick_and_place.py`.
- `demos/helpers.py` now hosts the shared `inject_free_joint` + `load_model_assets` (factored from the two demos that previously duplicated it).

**Key decisions**
- Adversarial policy: NaN/Inf measurements RAISE (runner throws `ValueError`) rather than silently corrupting control. Verified the Kalman filter currently propagates NaN — the integration test pins the raise behavior as the contract.
- Pick-and-place is feedforward (phase_list is a script of velocity setpoints), so it has no feedback controller/estimator; the schedule itself is the control.
